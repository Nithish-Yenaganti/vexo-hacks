Connect this phone

Your phone can reach the laptop.

On iPhone, motion access requires a trusted HTTPS connection.

  1. Download the laptop’s demo certificate
  2. Install the downloaded profile in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  3. In Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings, enable full trust for Grab the Screen local demo CA.
  4. Return here, open the receiver, and tap Enable motion.
